# The Puppeteer

**Unity • C# • 2D Platformer**

**The Puppeteer** is a 2D pixel-art platformer where the player takes the role of **The Dancer**, performing on a theater stage while confronting **The Puppeteer**, a mysterious figure who controls puppets and manipulates the performance from the shadows.

The game combines **platforming combat, mask-based abilities, and theatrical storytelling**, where the player must defeat enemies, collect magical masks, and free puppets from the Puppeteer’s control.

Developed during **Global Game Jam 2026**, inspired by the theme **“MASK.”**

---

# Game Overview

In **The Puppeteer**, the player performs on a theatrical stage while confronting a mysterious puppeteer who manipulates the world like a performance.

The audience watches as the show unfolds, but something darker hides behind the curtains.

The player must battle corrupted puppets and collect magical masks that grant new abilities.

Each section of the stage represents a new part of the performance, where the player must:

- Defeat puppet enemies  
- Collect magical masks  
- Avoid stage hazards  
- Progress through the theatrical environment  
- Confront the Puppeteer controlling the show  

As the game progresses, the stage becomes more chaotic and the Puppeteer’s control becomes stronger.

# Key Features

## Mask Ability System
Collect masks that grant special powers to the player.

## Puppet Enemies
Enemies controlled by the mysterious Puppeteer.

## Platformer Combat
Jump, dodge, and attack enemies while navigating the stage.

## Pixel Art Visual Style
Hand-crafted pixel environments and characters inspired by theatrical performances.

## Theater Theme
The entire game takes place on a dramatic stage performance where the player fights for control of the show.

---

# Characters

## The Dancer
**Playable Character**

A mysterious performer forced to participate in the Puppeteer’s show.

Elegant, agile, and determined to break free from the control of the stage.

Uses masks to gain new abilities and fight enemies.

---

## The Puppeteer
**Main Antagonist**

A shadowy figure controlling the puppets and manipulating the performance from behind the curtains.

Controls enemies and shapes the stage to challenge the player.

---

# Level Environment

## Theater Stage

The entire game takes place within a dramatic stage environment, including:

- Curtains and stage props  
- Puppet enemies  
- Mask power-ups  
- Hazardous stage elements  

The stage evolves as the performance progresses.
